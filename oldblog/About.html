This is an archive of Andrew Hussie's old blog. Not the blog linked to on the Secrets page, but the one that that one refers to as his "old blog". It originally appeared at http://www.andrewhussie.com, later moving to http://www.andrewhussie.com/blogs/, and has since been taken down. Therefore, a lot of it is missing. I got most of this from the Wayback Machine; other than that, the only places I've been able to find anything from it is Andrew's aforementioned slightly less old blog and a snippet chanced upon in the fora.

I tried to update the links and images from the Wayback Machine where possible; in particular, changing teamspecialolympics.com to andrewhussie.com often helped. When I was unable to find the destination of a link, either at the original address or elsewhere, I made it red. There were also a few emoticons that, as far as I know, are no longer with us (including, appropriately enough, graydead.gif). These each had alt text that seemed to be a textual representation of the emoticon; I made those the title text as well, so you can hover your mouse over the broken image to get an idea of what it was.

Notes by me are in square brackets.

If you have any suggestions or info or what have you, just PM me on the MSPA forums or contact me via my tumblr.
